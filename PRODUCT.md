# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Improving and competitive online chess players (roughly 1000–2200) who play on
chess.com and Lichess and want to get better, not just review one game. They
arrive after a session of games, in an analytical mindset, wanting to understand
patterns across their play: where they keep going wrong, which openings betray
them, and what to train next.

## Product Purpose

Tempo Chess connects a player's chess.com / Lichess accounts and analyzes their
whole history — not one game at a time. It records every blunder and mistake
accurately, explains each in plain language (the tactic missed, the idea skipped),
turns those mistakes into personalized puzzles, builds a per-player style
fingerprint, and recommends openings and study material matched to how the person
actually plays. Success = the player sees, in one glance, where their vision ends,
and has a concrete next thing to train.

## Brand Personality

Precise, honest, coach-like. Three words: **sharp, candid, calm.** It tells you
the truth about your play (you lost more than you won; here's why) without
scolding, and points forward. The voice of a strong second who has already done
the analysis.

## Anti-references

- Not a casual, gamified, badge-confetti app (not Duolingo-for-chess).
- Not a cluttered stat-dump with 40 numbers and no hierarchy.
- Not a generic SaaS marketing dashboard: no gradient hero-metric cards, no
  purple glow, no decorative motion.
- Not sugarcoated: it won't hide a losing record behind a vanity streak.

## Design Principles

1. **Honest instrumentation.** Show the real numbers, including the unflattering
   ones. Trust is the product.
2. **One glance, then depth.** The top of the screen answers "how am I doing and
   what should I fix"; detail is one scroll or click away.
3. **Every number earns its place.** No metric without a decision it informs.
4. **The tool disappears.** Earned familiarity over novelty; it should feel like
   a serious instrument, not a toy.
5. **Point forward.** Every weakness surfaced comes with a next action (a puzzle,
   a line to study, a game to watch).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum (AAA for primary metrics). Never encode meaning in color alone —
results and severities carry a label or shape as well as a hue (color-blind safe).
Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support; all count-ups and arc draws collapse to
static. Keyboard-navigable throughout.
